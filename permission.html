A tiny camera preview that floats above any page. Hold your shortcut, glance at yourself, and release to tuck it away.

About permissions:

This page lets you test the camera. The first time you use the shortcut on a website, Chrome will ask for camera access for that site. Choose Allow while visiting the site so it persists for that site. The test button below does not grant permission for other websites.

Permissions FAQ
Why do I only have to grant camera access once?
Glimp asks for camera access from its own extension page, not from each website you visit. Chrome ties the permission to that one page, so granting it here covers every site afterward — no per-site prompts.
Is that safe?
It's a deliberate trade-off. Since the permission is shared everywhere, any site could in theory reuse it silently instead of asking again. Nothing you capture is ever sent anywhere — photos and videos are saved straight to your downloads and never leave your browser.
The shortcut isn't opening the camera on a specific site — why?
Some sites configure their servers to block camera and microphone access for everything embedded in the page, including extensions. When that happens, Glimp closes quietly instead of showing an error — it's a restriction set by that site, not something granting permission again can fix.
How do I revoke access later?
Open chrome://settings/content/camera (and the microphone equivalent) and remove the Glimp extension's entry, or remove the extension entirely from chrome://extensions.
